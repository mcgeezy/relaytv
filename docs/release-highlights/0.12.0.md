## 🎬 New: unified multi-server Browse & friendly header actions

Browse all of your connected Jellyfin, Plex, and Emby servers together in a single library view with automatic cross-server deduplication that selects the lowest-latency source and merges watch progress. Header integration controls now use clear, action-oriented labels (`Browse`, `Add`, `TV`, `Link`) with a dedicated notification dialog.

## 📃 New: add entire YouTube playlists

Share a YouTube playlist from your phone and RelayTV automatically imports every
available video: Queue appends during playback, while Play starts the first video
and queues the rest in order. The web Link dialog also offers whole-playlist and
individual-video actions, with import status, cancellation, and request-ID retry
protection.

## ⏪ New: live stream DVR rewind

RelayTV now supports live stream DVR rewind within mpv's demuxer cache window.
Scrub backward through cached live broadcasts, monitor rewound delay times directly
on the player card and web remote, and tap the DVR badge or jump back to the live
edge at any time.
