## ⚡ Live controls that feel instant, everywhere

Press play, drag the scrubber, or change the volume and see every screen keep
up. RelayTV now brings faster live playback and status updates to the browser,
Home Assistant, Android, and the TV overlay over one resilient realtime
connection. Older clients and restrictive networks still work automatically —
RelayTV quietly falls back without asking you to configure a thing.

## 🔎 New: find it, request it, watch it with Seerr

Turn the RelayTV remote into the front door to your media library. Browse what
is trending, search for a movie or series, request exactly what you want, and
follow its progress without hopping between apps. When a title is ready,
RelayTV can send it straight to the TV after securely matching it to the active
Jellyfin server. Operators can offer a shared request identity or let everyone
connect their own account. See
[Seerr operations](https://github.com/mcgeezy/relaytv/blob/main/docs/SEERR_OPERATIONS.md).
