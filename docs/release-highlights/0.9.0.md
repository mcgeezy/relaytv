## 🔀 New: send the queue to another RelayTV device

RelayTV boxes can now find each other on your network and pass media between
them. **Send** in the queue header opens a device sheet: what is playing picks
up on the other TV at the same moment you left it, the queue goes with it, and
this room stops — then walk away. **Copy** does the same without touching this
room, so both TVs play. Pick exactly what travels from the list at the bottom
of the sheet. Devices show up under **Found nearby** over mDNS, or add one by
address; either way nothing is ever added or sent without you asking. See
[device sync operations](https://github.com/mcgeezy/relaytv/blob/main/docs/DEVICE_SYNC_OPERATIONS.md).
