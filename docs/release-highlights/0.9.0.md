## 🔀 New: send the queue to another RelayTV device

RelayTV boxes can now find each other on your network and pass media between
them. **Send** in the queue header opens a device sheet: copy the queue to
another room, move it there outright, or **hand off** what is playing so it
picks up on the other TV at the same moment you left it — then walk away.
Devices show up under **Found nearby** over mDNS, or add one by address; either
way nothing is ever added or sent without you asking. See
[device sync operations](https://github.com/mcgeezy/relaytv/blob/main/docs/DEVICE_SYNC_OPERATIONS.md).
