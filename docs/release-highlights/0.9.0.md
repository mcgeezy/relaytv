## 🔀 New: move what you are watching to another room

RelayTV boxes can now find each other on your network and pass playback between
them. **Send** in the queue header opens a device sheet: the other TV picks up
what is playing at the same moment you left it, takes the queue with it, and
this room stops — walk from the couch to the bedroom and keep watching.
**Copy** sends exactly the same thing but leaves this room alone, so both TVs
play together. A list at the bottom of the sheet shows what is playing and
everything queued, all selected — tap any of them to leave it behind. Devices
show up under **Found nearby** over mDNS, or add one by address; either way
nothing is ever added or sent without you asking. See
[device sync operations](https://github.com/mcgeezy/relaytv/blob/main/docs/DEVICE_SYNC_OPERATIONS.md).
