## 📹 New: direct RTSP & RTSPS camera stream playback

Play live `rtsp://` and `rtsps://` feeds from IP cameras, NVRs, local relays (`go2rtc`, Frigate, Scrypted, UniFi Protect), and doorbells directly on your TV through `/share`, the `/ui` Link dialog, or Home Assistant `play_temporary` interruptions with automatic resume. Streams start immediately in `mpv` over TCP with live DVR rewind support while keeping camera credentials redacted from public API payloads, logs, toasts, and peer transfers.
